# Fathom

Descend into a trench that begins almost completely black. Your Glimmerfin carries a pool of cool light; every plankton you graze makes it swell, exposing more of the maze—and more of you.

Sonar pours around corners and leaves a memory of corridors beyond your light. It can also betray you to the sound-hunting Gloamfin, while ink breaks the gaze of hunters that track brightness.

The Lanternjaw disguises itself as harmless amber drift, the eyeless Gloamfin listens, and the Flarefish floods the dark with its own dangerous light. Clear the maze, dive deeper, and face a larger hunting pack.
